Routing

The right specialist,
every single time.

One generalist model for everything? Nodal OS bets the other way: a team of specialists, with a fast router deciding who takes the job.

▸

Classification in milliseconds. A lightweight always-resident model reads each request and tags the kind of work: writing, numbers, legal reading, images, code.

▸

Hot-swap from NVMe. Models stream from the drive into GPU memory at NVMe speed — a swap takes seconds, not minutes, so switching specialists is invisible to your team.

▸

Grounded in your documents. Answers that need your facts pull from the indexed archive built at install time — quotes use your price list, letters use your templates.

▸

Nobody picks a model. Your staff see one chat box and one answer. The routing, loading, and grounding all happen under the hood.

Questions

Asked often

Is a local model actually good enough for real work?

For the tasks small businesses run daily — drafting, summarising, answering from your own documents, translating, classifying — modern open models are excellent, and because they're tuned to your business and your files, they're often more useful than a generic cloud chatbot. We'll demo on your real documents before you buy anything.

Why store many models instead of running one big one?

A single giant model needs giant hardware. A library of specialists on NVMe gets you better answers per dollar: the router loads a writing model for writing, a vision model for photos, a document model for your archive — each one the right tool, loaded in seconds.

What does the ongoing fee cover, exactly?

The Nodal OS subscription is the living part of the system: the routing software licence, monthly model library updates delivered to your NVMe drives, remote health monitoring, security patches, and priority support. The hardware is a one-time purchase; the subscription is what keeps it current.

Does the monitoring mean you can see our data?

No. Health monitoring reads machine vitals — temperatures, drive health, service status — never your prompts, documents, or answers. Content stays on your machine, full stop, and you can disable remote monitoring entirely if you prefer.

Do you need our data to set it up?

Only inside your building. Indexing and tuning happen on the machine itself during installation — we never take copies of your files off site.

Can the library change as our business changes?

Yes. The library is re-curated whenever you need — take on multilingual clients and we add translation depth; start doing site inspections and we add vision. Swaps ride along with the monthly updates at no extra cost.
