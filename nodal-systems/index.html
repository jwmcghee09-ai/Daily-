Security

Security isn't a feature here.
It's the architecture.

Cloud AI means sending customer records, contracts, and pricing to someone else's computer. A Nodal server removes that risk at the root: the intelligence lives in your building, and your data never leaves it.

Private by physics

Prompts, documents, and answers never cross the internet. There is no vendor to trust and no transit to intercept — the data simply doesn't leave.

No third-party eyes

No cloud provider logs your usage, no AI company trains on your files, no subpoena to a data centre can touch what's stored on your own machine.

Works offline

The system runs with the network cable unplugged. Internet outages, provider downtime, and model deprecations can't stop your team working.

Encrypted at rest

The NVMe drives holding your models and indexed documents are fully encrypted. A stolen drive is a brick, not a breach.

Per-user access

Local accounts for each staff member, with controls over who can query which documents. Payroll stays with the people who should see payroll.

Compliance-friendly

Client confidentiality, health records, financial data — keeping processing on-premise makes the privacy conversation with your regulator a short one.

The software

One box. Many minds.

Nodal OS keeps a library of specialised AI models parked on NVMe drives inside your server. When a task comes in, it picks the model built for that job and loads it in seconds — your team just asks, and the right specialist answers.

01 · Library

Models on NVMe

Chat, writing, document Q&A, code, vision, translation — a curated set of local models stored at full speed on your drives, sized to your machine.

02 · Router

Task-aware routing

The router reads each request, classifies the kind of work, and hot-loads the best model from the library. Nobody on your team ever picks a model.

03 · Your data

Tuned to your business

During install we index your documents, price lists, and templates on the machine itself — answers come back in your voice, with your facts.

04 · Ongoing

Alive via subscription

The ongoing Nodal OS fee keeps the library current: new and improved models arrive monthly, tested and tuned by us before they reach your drive.
